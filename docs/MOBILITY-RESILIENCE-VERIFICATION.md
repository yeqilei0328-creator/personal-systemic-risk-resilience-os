# Mobility Resilience Verification v0.1

Mobility is not “owning a vehicle”.

It is the ability to move required people/cargo across a defined mission while energy, roads, communications and maintenance may be degraded.

## Model

1. Mission
2. Vehicle
3. Energy/replenishment
4. Route diversity
5. Maintenance/spares
6. Offline navigation
7. Degraded mission test

## Key rules

- brochure range does not count as demonstrated range;
- two roads sharing one chokepoint are one route group;
- two energy sources sharing one critical dependency may be one group;
- online navigation alone is fragile;
- a vehicle without maintenance/tires/spares is a decaying asset.

## Preparation order

Low-regret now:
- keep normal maintenance current;
- inspect tires;
- keep documents and offline maps;
- define who/cargo a resilient mission must carry;
- identify basic tools/spares;
- avoid routinely letting all mobility energy sources approach empty.

Wait for mission evidence before:
- buying a second “survival vehicle”;
- expensive modifications;
- large fuel storage;
- specialized off-road equipment.

The objective is mission continuity, not automotive cosplay.
